# Shipping cost optimization with dangerous goods penalty
Did you ever wonder how to optimally pack parcels, serving customers with various subscription sizes while avoiding a dangerous goods shipping penalty fee?
No? Well, me neither. Let's investigate how its done anyways!
Stay with me, its worth it: we will reduce shipping cost by 55% just by intelligently exploiting the degressive cost-per-weight structure in today's CEP sector and reduce our cost to serve by up to 20%.
We will rediscover the knapsack problem along the way and prove optimal bin allocation alone yields lower-than-industry cost to serve.
Enough for the hook. Let's do it!
